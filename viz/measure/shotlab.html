2 · Battery-sag compensation

derived

Fits steady wheel rpm = a·(power·volts) + b from your robot log (rows on spin-up ramps are skipped). Then the power to hold a target rpm at any battery voltage is (target − b) / (a·volts).

What the model cannot say: it has no contact physics. Any touch of the lip counts as a hit and ends the shot, so it cannot show a ball that kisses the lip and still drops in, and it has no "impulse torque" from a lip contact. It can only compare clean shots that pass close to the lip with clean shots that pass farther away. Robustness = the share of clean shots in a Gaussian window of ASSUMED launch scatter (speed 1.5 %, angle 0.4°, 1 σ) around each setting, in a local scan (speed ±2 m/s, angle ±6°) centred on the explorer's most forgiving setting; the two picks and 20 shots across the clearance range also get a 3D Monte Carlo that adds 0.5° heading scatter (Wilson 95 % interval). Energy = ½·m·v² at cell entry, m 0.025 kg. None of this is a measured repeatability.
