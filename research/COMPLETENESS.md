# PM-001 completeness critique of the literature work

Written 2026-10-30 by the completeness agent. It checks `research/LITERATURE_SYNTHESIS.md` (called "the synthesis" below) and `research/params_lit.json` against each thing the user asked for. It also checks them against the 25-source list in `RESEARCH_PAPERS.md` and the requirements in HANDOFF.md §0, §4 and §5.

Numbers marked **(computed)** are my own arithmetic with m 24.95 g, R 35.5 mm, A 3.96e-3 m², ρ 1.20 and ν 1.5e-5. No source provided them.

**Verdict:** the synthesis is strong on aerodynamic proxies and launcher theory. It is weak or silent on four things:
- the near-apex regime;
- what "useful" spin means for the real HIVE corridor;
- collisions inside the CELL;
- side-by-side and across-field evidence.

None of the 25 sources contain a POLLEN measurement. Every gap below can be closed by a cheap POLLEN test, and for most gaps that test is the only fix.

## 2. Unsupported or mis-graded claims

1. **Synthesis §3, "non-Magnus hole force … averages out" at 30–50 rev/s.** `research/sweeps/sweep_aero.md` itself calls this "Hypothesis, not established". The synthesis states it as a finding. Mark it D, or test it by comparing scatter between spinning and non-spinning shots in T1.
2. **Synthesis §6, "Magnus lift therefore cannot rescue a shot on its own".** This generalises from one generic target and entry angle. The unfavourable-side question is about corridor clearance, where 5–12 cm of apex rise could matter at the edge of the corridor. It stays unsupported until the corridor run in §1 is done.
3. **Synthesis §6, "Its main lever is how the ball behaves after impact".** No collision simulation backs this. It is a hypothesis.
4. **Synthesis §7.4, e_n bounce-out rule of thumb.** There is no calculation or source.
5. **Synthesis §6 numbers come from an unsaved scratchpad script**, so they cannot be reproduced. Save the script or regenerate the numbers in src/.
6. **`params_lit.json` `contact.e_t.grade = "A"`.** The value is grade A for tennis balls, but applying it to POLLEN is a judgement, so the field should be **B**. `spin_retained_near_normal` is in the same position: the formula is grade A, the α and e_x it uses are not.
7. **`spin_decay_k.grade = "B"`.** β comes from golf and baseball. A perforated shell may pump air through its holes, so for POLLEN this is **D**.
8. **The Steyn grade B rests on the preprint.** The version of record returned 403 and was not read. The status line in `params_lit.json` reads as if it was ("peer-reviewed VoR; preprint content checked"). Change it to "VoR unread; preprint read".
9. **Sakai 2016 is grade C, but Miyazaki 2017 (EJP, peer-reviewed) is the same measurement campaign.** If the Miyazaki full text confirms the values, the dip table can go up to B. It is still not an independent second source.
10. **The `Cl.high` cap of 0.40** is above every measured backspin Cl at this Re: the smooth-sphere maximum is 0.33, and Vilumbrales's 0.35–0.40 is for a non-spinning rough sphere. Keep it as an explicit optimistic bound and label it that way.
11. **N(δ) E range of 0.2–1.2 GPa and the pickleball drop-spec COR of 0.62–0.66** are both "from memory". The second can be closed from the USA Pickleball Equipment Standards Manual, which gives the drop height and bounce window. That would still be a pickleball value, not POLLEN.
