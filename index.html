Shot window at this position

What this is. The squares and the green fan come straight from the PM-001 model grid (src/shooter_spec.py: 111 speeds × 261 angles × 24 distances × 5 heights per case). Each shot is a 2D flight in the HIVE's tipping plane, through the centre of the cell, with drag, spin lift capped at a maximum, and spin decay. The paths drawn here are re-flown in your browser with the same equations. The HIVE section is from the official field CAD; tilt 30° is confirmed.

What it is not. No POLLEN drag, spin lift, bounce or launcher grip has been measured. The air cases are literature ranges (pickleball and smooth-sphere data), not POLLEN facts. A shot that touches the HIVE stops here; a bounce can still score (see docs/05). The second HIVE and the A-frame legs are drawn for context only. One clean simulated shot says nothing about repeatability: the tolerance shown is how far speed and angle can drift and still go in.
