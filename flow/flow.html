Layers

Added 3D layers, on top of the ones above. PREDICTED (CFD model output). Tracers are massless specks carried by the CFD air, released upstream, all over the ball and in the wake. Lift pairs: a line of tracers released together; the orange one passes above the ball, the blue one below.

Flow

Instant shows the turbulent wake's vortices; averaged shows the mean flow. In Instant the tubes are vortex cores (Q-criterion) of one moment of a turbulent (LES) wake, and the blotchy slice is the same turbulence: not a model error.
